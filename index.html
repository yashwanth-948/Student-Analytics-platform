Score Methodology

Understand how the prototype calculates success scores and risk

Student Success Score (0–100)

The score is a weighted indicator of student progress. A higher score indicates stronger performance across the selected academic and engagement measures.

FactorWeight
Academic (CGPA 20, Backlogs 8, Subject marks 7)35%
Attendance (Overall 12, Weakest subject 8)20%
Engagement (LMS logins 6, Assignments 8, Clubs/events/certs 6)20%
Placement (Aptitude 5, Coding 5, Mock interviews 4, Technical 3, Soft skills 3)20%
Feedback (Student 3, Faculty 2)5%

Risk rules used in this prototype

  • Academic risk: CGPA below 6, attendance below 75%, 3 or more backlogs, or academic pillar below 45%.
  • Placement risk: placement readiness pillar below 50%.
  • High risk: score below 52, attendance below 65%, or both risk flags with a score below 60. Medium: score below 65. Low: everything else.

These are demonstration rules, not a validated predictive AI model. Use approved institutional data and validate the thresholds before real-world decisions.
